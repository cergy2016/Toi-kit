@Single|Article|Local Farm Market Moves Indoors
Greenfield — The Saturday Farmers' Market, a popular fixture in Town Square for twenty-two years, will move to the former Hartley Hardware building starting in November. Organizers made the decision after a survey found that nearly sixty percent of vendors wanted a location that would stay open through the winter.
"Outdoor markets get quieter when the weather turns cold," said market manager Josie Brandt. "With heat and a roof, our vendors can keep selling, and customers can shop in comfort." The building has about fifty stalls, compared with thirty-five in the square, and a small kitchen where vendors can prepare samples. A parking area behind the building will hold eighty cars, which is more than double the current street parking.
The market will keep its Saturday hours, from eight to one. Starting in December, it will also open on Wednesday evenings. Vendors interested in a stall can apply online until October 15.

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Riverton General Hospital announced today that its new surgical wing will be named after Dr. Samuel Achebe, who served as chief surgeon for thirty-four years. The wing, which is scheduled to open on September 1, includes eight operating rooms and a recovery area with forty beds.
"Dr. Achebe trained more than two hundred surgeons during his career," said hospital director Louise Marchetti. "We are honored to recognize his commitment to our community." The project cost $62 million, of which $15 million came from private donations. The new rooms feature advanced imaging equipment that will allow doctors to perform some operations through smaller incisions, shortening recovery times.
A public ceremony will take place on August 28 at 10 a.m. Parking will be available at the nearby Millstream Mall, with free shuttle service to the hospital. For media inquiries, contact Kyle Brandt at 555-0123.

Baker Publishes First Recipe Book
Elena Moretti, owner of Moretti's Bakery in Dunmore, will publish her first recipe book next month.
